Logbook servicing that keeps your new car warranty valid

We service your car to the manufacturer's schedule for its age and kilometres, use parts of suitable quality and stamp your logbook. Under Australian Consumer Law, that keeps your new car warranty intact without going back to the dealer.

Tyres and wheels

Wheel alignment and balancing to stop uneven tyre wear

Poor alignment wears tyres out early and makes the car pull. Balancing stops vibration through the steering wheel at highway speed. Both are worth doing whenever you fit new tyres.

Signs you need it

  • Car pulls to one side
  • Steering wheel off-centre on a straight road
  • Vibration at 80 to 110 km/h
  • Tyres worn on the inside or outside edge

Book it

  • With every new set of tyres
  • After hitting a kerb or pothole
  • After suspension or steering work

Brakes, suspension and steering repairs

These three systems decide how well your car stops and handles. We inspect pads, rotors, brake fluid, shocks, bushes and steering parts, then explain what needs doing.

Registration

Pink slips and safety checks for NSW rego

In NSW, cars more than five years old need an eSafety inspection report, known as a pink slip, to renew registration. The inspection must be done within 42 days before your rego is due.

What's inspected

  • Tyres and wheels
  • Brakes and steering
  • Lights, indicators and horn
  • Windscreen, wipers and seatbelts

Good to know

  • Results go to Transport for NSW electronically
  • Pink slips at selected stores, including Kurri Kurri
  • Blue slips at Belmont, Cessnock and Kurri Kurri

All FAQs
What's included when I buy tyres from O'Neills?

Fitting, standard wheel balancing and new valves are included at no extra cost. Wheel alignment is a separate service, and we recommend it with every new set.

How long does tyre fitting take?

Around 30 to 45 minutes for a set of four, including balancing and a safety check. Allow extra time if you add a wheel alignment.

When do my tyres need replacing?

The legal minimum tread depth in NSW is 1.5mm, which is level with the tread wear indicators in the grooves. Replace tyres sooner if you see cracks, bulges or uneven wear, and have tyres older than 5 years checked even if the tread looks fine.

Will a logbook service at O'Neills affect my new car warranty?

No. Under Australian Consumer Law you don't need to service at the dealer. Your warranty stays valid when a qualified mechanic follows the manufacturer's schedule using parts of suitable quality, and we record every service in your logbook.

Can I pay for tyres in instalments?

Yes. You can pay with Afterpay, Zip or Humm, as well as Visa, Mastercard, American Express, EFTPOS and cash. See payment options for details.

Do you fit truck, earthmover and farm tyres?

Yes. Our Beresfield TME store at 2 Babilla Cl, Beresfield handles truck, bus, earthmover, forklift and tractor tyres. Call (02) 4966 0444.

Can I get a pink slip at O'Neills?

Yes, at selected stores including Kurri Kurri. In NSW, cars more than five years old need a pink slip (eSafety inspection) within 42 days before rego renewal. Call your nearest store to check it offers pink slips and book a time.

What's the difference between a pink slip and a blue slip?

A pink slip is the yearly safety check for registration renewal. A blue slip (AUVIS inspection) is needed to register a vehicle that is unregistered, coming from interstate or has been modified. Blue slips are available at our Belmont, Cessnock and Kurri Kurri stores.
