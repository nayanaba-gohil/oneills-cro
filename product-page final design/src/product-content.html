Questions about this tyre

Is this the right size for my car?

This size is listed for the Subaru WRX. Check that the size on your tyre placard or current tyres reads 245/35ZR19 or 245/35R19. If you're not sure, search by your car's make and model or call your nearest store.

What do 89 and W mean?

89 is the load index: each tyre can carry up to 580 kg. W is the speed rating: up to 270 km/h. Your new tyres should match or exceed the load index and speed rating on your tyre placard.

What's the difference between ZR and R in the size?

ZR marks a radial tyre rated for speeds above 240 km/h. The W in the service description (89W) sets the actual limit, which is 270 km/h. A 245/35R19 89W is the same size and speed rating.

Does the price include fitting?

Yes. The price includes GST, fitting, standard wheel balancing, new tyre valves and eco disposal of your old tyres. Wheel alignment is a separate service, and we recommend it with every new set.

When should I replace my tyres?

The legal minimum tread depth in NSW is 1.5 mm across the tread. Tread wear indicators in the grooves show when a tyre reaches it. Replace sooner if you see cracks, bulges or uneven wear. Any O'Neills store can check your tyres for you.

Do I need to replace all four tyres?

On all-wheel-drive cars like the WRX, a big difference in tread depth between tyres can strain the drivetrain, so most makers recommend replacing all four together. On other cars, replace at least the pair on one axle. Our team can measure your tread depth and advise.

How long does fitting take?

Around 30 to 45 minutes for a set of four, including balancing. Allow extra time if you add a wheel alignment.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. You can pay with Afterpay, Zip or humm, as well as card. See payment options for details.

Will it fit my car?

This size is listed for the Subaru WRX. Before you order, check the size printed on your current tyres or on the tyre placard on the driver's door jamb. It should read 245/35ZR19 or 245/35R19, with a load index of 89 or higher and a speed rating of W or higher.
