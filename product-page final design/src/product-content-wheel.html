About the ROH Apollo

The Apollo is a clean, minimalist spoke wheel from the exclusive LITEFORGED series, made for 4WD and off-road use. Flow-formed construction reduces weight for better performance without compromising strength. It is available in 6x139.7 fitments only, in a Machined or Matte Black finish. See all ROH wheels, or browse 18 inch wheels and 17 inch wheels.

Is it the right wheel for you?

  • You drive a 4WD or ute with a 6x139.7 stud pattern. It is common on many dual-cab utes and 4WDs, so check your car before you order.
  • You want a lighter flow-formed wheel without giving up strength.
  • You want to choose the offset to suit your build. A lower offset sits the wheel further out.

Questions about this wheel

What does 18x9 6x139.7 +25 mean?

18 is the rim diameter in inches and 9 is the rim width in inches. 6x139.7 is the stud pattern: six studs on a 139.7 mm circle (PCD). +25 is the offset in millimetres. See the Will it fit? tab for a diagram.

Will these wheels fit my car?

The Apollo is made for a 6x139.7 stud pattern, so your car needs six studs on a 139.7 mm circle. The centre bore, the offset and brake clearance also need to suit. Your tyre placard lists tyre sizes, not wheel fitment, so if you are not sure, ask one of our stores to check before you order.

What is wheel offset?

Offset is the distance in millimetres between the wheel centreline and the face that mounts to the hub. A positive offset (+) means the mounting face sits toward the outside of the wheel, so the wheel sits further in. A lower offset pushes the wheel further out. The Apollo comes in +12, +25 and +40 mm.

Can I keep my current tyres?

Only if the tyre size suits the new rim diameter and width. If you change the rim diameter, you need tyres to match. Ask one of our stores and we will check your tyres against the wheels you want.

Do you fit and balance the wheels?

Yes, at any O'Neills store. You choose the store and fitting time at checkout.

Will it fit my car?

Wheels fit by stud pattern, offset, centre bore and brake clearance. The Apollo has a 6x139.7 stud pattern, so check that your car has six studs on a 139.7 mm circle, then choose an offset to suit. Your tyre placard lists tyre sizes, not wheel offset, so if you are not sure, ask a store to check before you order.
